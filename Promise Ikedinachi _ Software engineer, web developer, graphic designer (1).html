About me

Hi, I’m Promise Ikedinachi.

I help business owners and founders build websites and web apps that work well on the internet, and I design logos, posters and brand visuals that make businesses stand out.

I am a computer science student. When I was growing up, I didn’t have a good chance to learn computer science, so I taught myself, bought courses, and I made it perfect with consistency.

That is how I came about my software engineering skills, and I have been recommended. I worked with many companies.

Beyond code, I’m a graphic designer. I create logos, brand identities and social media flyers that help businesses look professional and get remembered.
